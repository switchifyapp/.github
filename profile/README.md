# Switchify

**Control your phone, tablet and computer with a switch.**

Switchify is switch access for Android, Windows and Mac, built for people with mobility impairments who can't use a touchscreen. Item, point and radar scanning, camera gestures as switches, gesture patterns, a menu built for switch users, and PC control for your computer.

[**Get Switchify on Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.enaboapps.switchify) · [**Switchify PC for Windows and Mac**](https://github.com/switchifyapp/switchify-pc/releases) · [**theswitchify.app**](https://www.theswitchify.app)

---

## Two apps, one idea

- **Switchify for Android** is the switch access app itself. Free on Google Play, Android 10 or later, Pro is optional. PC control is built in: mouse, typing, window controls and Switch Forwarding over Bluetooth.
- **Switchify PC for Windows and Mac** is the desktop app your Android device pairs with. It approves pairing, moves the pointer, types, manages windows, and turns forwarded switches into keys, clicks and shortcuts. Free, no account needed. Version 1.0 is a release candidate on GitHub for Windows and Apple-silicon Mac.
